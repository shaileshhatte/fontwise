Welcome to Fontwise

Make any web page easier to read: a better font, comfortable spacing and calm colours, in one click.

  1. Pin Fontwise

    Open the extensions menu (the puzzle icon) and pin Fontwise to the toolbar.

  2. Open any article

    Click the Fontwise icon and choose “Best for reading”, or pick a font and tune the details.

  3. Choose where it applies

    Keep changes to this tab, remember them for the site, or use them everywhere.

What you can do

  • Readable fonts you already have, plus Atkinson Hyperlegible, Lexend, Literata and OpenDyslexic built in
  • Size, line, letter, word and paragraph spacing
  • Light, sepia, dark and high-contrast themes with a contrast check
  • Focus mode: just the article, nothing else
  • A reading ruler that keeps your place
  • Automatic dark colours at night

Private by design

Fontwise makes no network requests and collects no data. Your settings stay in your browser.
